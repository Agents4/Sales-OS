# Studio prompts: method coach

## Audio Overview
"Explain how Strategic Selling, Conceptual Selling and LAMP fit together for a seller with a complex deal. Use one worked example from the sources. Finish with the five most common mistakes."

## Report
"Write a one-page guide to building a Blue Sheet from messy notes. Include the order of work, what to leave Unknown, and the checks before sharing with a manager."

## Quiz
"Create 12 scenario questions on buying influences, response modes, Win-Results and red flags. Include explanations that point to the source."

## Flashcards
"Create flashcards for the glossary terms, with the canonical meaning on the back and a common misuse."

## Slide deck
"Create a 10-slide training deck: the three sheets, the four buying influences, response modes, Win-Results, red flags and the forecast gates."

## Mind map
"Map the method: the three sheets, what each answers, the questions they ask, and how they link."
