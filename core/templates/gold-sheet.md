# Gold Sheet: <Account> / <Field of Play>

> **Fidelity key.** **[C]** canonical LAMP element. **[V]** practitioner convention. **[X]** Sales-OS extension.
> LAMP has four phases: Data Gathering, Situation Appraisal, Strategy Planning, Action Planning. The Gold Sheet reads left to right: where we are, where we should go, how we get there. It is a living document, reviewed every 60-90 days and on trigger events.
