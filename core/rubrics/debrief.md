# Rubric: call debrief

| # | Criterion | Pass looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Result vs plan** | States whether the Best Action Commitment, the Minimum Acceptable Action or neither was achieved, quoting the evidence |
| 2 | **Facts separated from impressions** | New facts have a source (timestamp or quote); impressions are labelled I |
| 3 | **Concept** | The buyer's Concept in their words, and what changed from the hypothesis |
| 4 | **Mode and support changes** | Each change has old value, new value, the evidence, and confidence. One remark is Low confidence |
| 5 | **Win-Results** | Only what was actually said or clearly shown. Work-relevant. Nothing invented |
| 6 | **New people and signals** | New names, roles, process steps, timelines, reorganization, procurement or security gates captured and turned into unknowns or red flags |
| 7 | **Red flags** | Created and resolved flags listed; each new flag has a dated, owned action |
| 8 | **Commitments** | What we owe, what they owe, by when, written as actions |
| 9 | **Follow-up** | Draft follow-up (not sent), buyer-safe, restating agreed next steps only |
| 10 | **Blue Sheet diff** | Specific edits proposed: field, old, new, reason. Applied only with approval or per user instruction |
| 11 | **Boundary** | Nothing internal-only appears in the follow-up draft |
| 12 | **Honesty** | The debrief says where the call went worse than hoped |

## Fail conditions

Any invented quote, any mode change on a single ambiguous remark presented as fact, any internal-only item in the buyer-facing draft, or a BAC marked achieved without evidence.
