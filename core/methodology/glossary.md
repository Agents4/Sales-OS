# Glossary

Tags: **[C]** canonical, **[V]** convention, **[X]** Sales-OS extension.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Above the funnel** [C] | Suspects not yet qualified. |
| **Action plan** [C] | Dated, owned actions that target red flags or build on strengths. |
| **Additional Key Player** [C] | LAMP role for anyone else influential in the Field of Play. |
| **Anti-Sponsor** [C] | A Strategic Player who opposes you or your Sponsors. |
| **Attitude question** [C] | A Conceptual Selling question that surfaces feelings, priorities and personal stakes. |
| **Basic issue question** [C] | A question that reaches the underlying issue and the consequence of inaction. |
| **Best Action Commitment (BAC)** [C] | The most the buyer is realistically ready to do next, specific and dated. |
| **Best Few** [C] | The opportunities closest to closing that deserve full planning. |
| **Blue Sheet** [C] | Strategic Selling's one-opportunity plan. |
| **Buyer-facing / internal-only** [X] | Whether content may be shared with the customer. Modes, Win-Results, support ratings, red flags, Coach identity and competitive assessments are internal-only. |
| **Buy-Sell Hierarchy** [C] | Five-level scale of how the customer values the supplier. |
| **Buying influence** [C] | A person who influences the decision. Four kinds: Economic, User, Technical, Coach. |
| **Charter Statement** [C] | The mutual statement of where the relationship should go (LAMP). |
| **Coach** [C] | An internal guide who is credible, wants you to win and gives you information you cannot get elsewhere. |
| **Colleague test** [V] | Could a colleague say on the date whether the SSO was hit? |
| **Concept** [C] | The buyer's mental picture of the solved problem. |
| **Confirmation question** [C] | Verifies what you believe so the call starts from shared ground. |
| **Critical Vulnerability** [C] | A weakness that would cripple your position if left unaddressed (LAMP). |
| **Degree of influence** [C] | High, Medium or Low weight in this decision. |
| **Economic Buyer (EB)** [C] | The one person with final approval and access to discretionary funds. |
| **Evidence tag** [X] | F fact, I inference, A assumption, U unknown. |
| **Even Keel** [C] | Response mode: sees no gap; satisfied. |
| **Field of Play** [C] | The segment of a large account that is the focus of the plan. |
| **Focus investment** [C] | A resource commitment that helps the Field of Play act on its opportunities. |
| **Gold Sheet** [C] | LAMP's account plan. |
| **Green Sheet** [C] | Conceptual Selling's one-conversation plan. |
| **Growth** [C] | Response mode: sees a gap to a better future and is open to change. |
| **In the funnel** [C] | Qualified and actively worked. |
| **Minimum Acceptable Action (MAA)** [C] | The least that still advances the sale. |
| **New information question** [C] | Learns what you do not know. |
| **Overconfident** [C] | Response mode: believes things are better than they are. |
| **Red flag** [C] | Anything missing, unknown, uncertain or changing that could keep you from winning. |
| **Re-walk** [C] | Review the whole sheet again, not just add to it. |
| **Response mode** [C] | Growth, Trouble, Even Keel or Overconfident, per person per issue. |
| **Result** [C] | Measurable business outcome of the purchase. |
| **Single Sales Objective (SSO)** [C] | One sentence of what you want from the opportunity and by when. |
| **Sponsor** [C] | LAMP role: promotes your relationship and counters competitors. |
| **Status quo** [C] | The "do nothing" alternative; always included in the competition. |
| **Stop investment** [C] | A decision to discontinue an investment that no longer earns its return. |
| **Strategic Coach** [C] | LAMP role: helps you understand the Field of Play and its decisions. |
| **Strategic Player** [C] | A person in the Field of Play assigned a LAMP role. |
| **Strategic Strength** [C] | What you bring that the Field of Play values and competitors cannot easily match. |
| **Support rating** [V] | -5 to +5 estimate of how strongly a person favors the SSO over alternatives. |
| **Technical Buyer** [C] | Screens against specifications; can say no, cannot say yes. |
| **Trouble** [C] | Response mode: sees a problem now; wants relief. |
| **User Buyer** [C] | Lives with the result; judges the impact on their own work. |
| **Win** [C] | The personal payoff a buyer gets. |
| **Win-Results** [C] | A person's Win and Result together. |
