# Fidelity ledger

Purpose: be able to tell a client, precisely, what is Miller Heiman and what is not. Confidence reflects how many independent sources agree. Basis points to `docs/research-notes.md`. Reviewed 2026-10-17. Re-verify against official materials before a client rollout.

Status: **C** canonical, **V** convention, **X** Sales-OS extension. Confidence: **H** high, **M** medium, **L** low.

## Sales-OS extensions

Evidence tags F/I/A/U and Source/Confidence columns; unknowns register; decision-process and required-proof table; change log; lint rules and codes; buyer-facing redaction rules; privacy rules for Win-Results; forecast hygiene gates; signals library; account packs for Gemini Notebook; the skill library, adapters and tooling.

## Naming and rights

Strategic Selling, Conceptual Selling, Large Account Management Process, LAMP and the Blue, Green and Gold Sheet names belong to their owner (Miller Heiman Group, part of Korn Ferry). This repository contains an original synthesis, not the official worksheets or training content. Before marketing a deployment as "Miller Heiman" or distributing official forms, confirm licensing. Clients already licensed for the official methodology should be able to substitute their own scales and form wording; the templates are designed for that.
