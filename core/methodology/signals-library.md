# Signals library

All of this is **[X] Sales-OS heuristics**, not Miller Heiman doctrine. Use it to *notice* candidates in notes and transcripts, then record them as **inference (I)** or **assumption (A)** until the buyer's own words or behavior confirm them. Never convert a cue into a fact on its own. Quote the buyer; do not paraphrase into certainty.

## Response mode cues

| Mode | Language that suggests it | Behavior that suggests it | Look-alike to rule out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Growth** | "We want to expand to...", "next phase", "we are building toward...", "what could we do if...", talks in targets and timelines | Brings others into the conversation, shares plans, asks about what is possible | Polite curiosity with no budget or owner |
| **Trouble** | "We are struggling with...", "we got burned by...", "audit finding", "customers are complaining", deadlines and consequences named | Urgency, asks about speed and risk, willing to move before the usual process | A complaint about someone else's problem |
| **Even Keel** | "It works fine", "we have a vendor", "not a priority this year", "send me something", calm and general | Slow replies, no owner named, meetings get rescheduled | Satisfaction that hides a Trouble the person does not own |
| **Overconfident** | "We are ahead of plan", "we have this covered", "no issues", dismisses risk | Metrics cited are old or selective; others in the account disagree; reluctant to share data | Genuinely strong performance (check the data) |

Rules: mode is per person and per issue. A person can be Growth about revenue and Even Keel about your category. Record the issue.

## Win cues (work-relevant only)

| Win type | Cues |
|---|---|
| Recognition or advancement | "The board is looking at this", "this is my first year in the role", "I would like to show..." |
| Risk reduction or security | "I cannot afford another miss", "who owns it if it breaks", "last time it went wrong" |
| Control or autonomy | "We need to own this", "no black boxes", asks about configuration and data access |
| Ease or peace of mind | "I am tired of firefighting", "my team is stretched", "just make it simple" |
| Growth or learning | "I would like my team to learn...", interest in how it works |

Do not record or speculate on anything outside work: health, family, finances beyond the business role, politics, religion, personal characteristics. If a seller volunteers such detail, leave it out of the sheet.

## Coach tests: evidence to look for

| Test | Evidence that it passes | Evidence that it fails |
|---|---|---|
| Credibility in this account | Others defer to their view; they can get meetings; reports into or near the decision path | Not listened to; peripheral to the decision |
| Wants you to win | They have a Win tied to your success; they coach proactively; they prepare you for objections | Friendly but indifferent; never offers help unprompted |
| Gives information you cannot get elsewhere | Budget cycle, who really decides, competitor status, unwelcome news | Repeats what is on the website or what you already knew |

A Coach who has never given you bad news is untested. Ask for it: "What would worry you most if you were in my seat?"

## Red flag triggers in notes and transcripts

- A new name appears who was not on the map (procurement, legal, finance, security, a new executive).
- "Let me check with..." about a person you have not met.
- "We need to loop in..." later in the cycle than expected.
- Fewer people on the calls than before, or calls delegated downward.
- A silent period after a proposal or demo.
- A reorganization, hiring freeze, acquisition rumor or leadership change.
- A date moves, a stakeholder skips the meeting, or a data request goes unanswered.
- "We are also talking to..." without detail.
- A confident claim from a Coach that cannot be tied to a source.

## Evidence standard

| Tag | Meaning | Allowed basis |
|---|---|---|
| **F** fact | Directly stated or observed | A dated source: call, email, document, CRM record. Quote it. |
| **I** inference | Reasonably concluded from facts | Name the facts it rests on. |
| **A** assumption | Believed but untested | Mark it and write the question that would test it. |
| **U** unknown | No information | Write the question and who can answer. |

Record the source for every F. Prefer the buyer's exact words. Mark confidence High, Medium or Low. A single remark is Low confidence.
