# Complementary frameworks

**[X] Sales-OS perspective.** These are other frameworks you will meet in client organizations. They are not Miller Heiman and this is our mapping, not an official one. Use it to translate, not to merge.

## What Miller Heiman is best at

Miller Heiman is a *planning and relationship-mapping* methodology. It tells you who matters, how ready each person is to change, what each wants, what could go wrong, and how to run each conversation. It is weaker on explicit buyer decision criteria, quantified business case and mutual timelines, so Sales-OS adds a decision-process table and a place for required proof.

## Mapping

| Framework | What it asks | Where it lines up | Where it differs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MEDDICC / MEDDPICC** (Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Identify pain, Champion, Competition; "P" paper process) | Qualification checklist | Metrics = Result; Economic Buyer = Economic Buyer; Identify pain = Trouble mode and Basic issue; Competition = Competition; Decision criteria and process = Sales-OS decision-process table | A Champion sells for you inside; a Coach advises you. The same person can be both, but they are different jobs. MEDDICC has no Win concept and no mode. |
| **BANT** (Budget, Authority, Need, Timing) | Early lead qualification | Use above or at the edge of the funnel | Too thin for complex deals. Authority is not the same as the Economic Buyer's veto. |
| **SPIN** (Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-payoff) | Question sequence for discovery | Situation = confirmation; Problem and Implication = new information and basic issue; Need-payoff = commitment and Concept | SPIN is a question technique; Conceptual Selling adds the Concept and the commitment structure. |
| **Challenger** (Teach, Tailor, Take control) | Commercial insight that reframes the buyer's thinking | Teach = giving information that moves an Even Keel or Overconfident buyer; Tailor = tailoring to Win-Results by role | Challenger starts with the seller's insight; Conceptual Selling starts with the buyer's Concept. Use insight *after* the Concept is understood. |
| **Value selling / ROI models** | Quantify the business case | The quantified Result | Add the Win, otherwise the model persuades no one personally. |
| **Mutual action plan** | Shared timeline to signature | Built from the Blue Sheet's action plan and decision process | Buyer-facing: must exclude all internal analysis (see `operating-rules.md`). |

## How a client with MEDDICC uses Sales-OS

Keep MEDDICC as the *inspection* language in the forecast call and CRM fields; use the Blue Sheet as the *working* plan. The Blue Sheet fills the MEDDICC fields and goes further on people. `crm-contract.md` maps the fields.
