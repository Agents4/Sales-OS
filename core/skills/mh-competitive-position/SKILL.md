# Competitive position

Position is a judgment about how each buying influence sees the alternatives, not how we see ourselves. Include the status quo (do nothing) and an internal build as competitors every time.

Read `references/operating-rules.md` and `references/capabilities.md`. Use `references/competitor.md` as the adversarial lens and the competition section of `references/strategic-selling.md` for the method.

## When to use

- A named competitor is in the deal.
- The risk is no decision or a delay.
- The user asks how to counter an alternative or what to say about a rival.

## Inputs

The Blue Sheet, what the buyer has said about alternatives, any competitor materials the user has, and `workspace/context-library/competitors/` if it exists. If web access exists you may research public facts about a competitor; cite URLs and mark claims you could not verify.

## Procedure

1. **List the alternatives:** each named competitor, status quo, internal build, and any partner or hybrid.
2. **For each influence, record perception** with evidence: which alternative is the buyer leaning to, and why. Unknown is a valid answer.
3. **Assess each alternative** from the buyer's view: strengths, weaknesses, incumbent advantages, risks the buyer sees.
4. **Position:** front-runner, in the mix, behind or unknown, with the evidence and the biggest uncertainty.
5. **Run the competitor lens.** What are the best three arguments against us, which influence is most exposed, and what true and damaging thing might be said?
6. **Plan counters** per influence: evidence, access and timing. Include what to stop saying (unsupported claims, jargon, attacks on competitors that reduce credibility).
7. **Do-nothing plan.** What does inaction cost, in the buyer's terms and numbers? How can that be made visible without scaring or pressuring them?
8. **Record** results in the Blue Sheet's competition table through `mh-blue-sheet` update rules.

## Output

A table of alternatives and perception by influence, a position statement with confidence, the three arguments against us, the counters and the next two actions. File save: `workspace/outputs/reviews/YYYY-MM-DD-<opportunity>-competitive.md`.

## Quality gate

1. Perception comes from the buyer's words or is marked inferred.
2. Public claims about competitors are sourced; no rumor stated as fact; no disparagement in buyer-facing material.
3. Internal competitive assessments stay internal.
4. Status quo and internal build are present.
