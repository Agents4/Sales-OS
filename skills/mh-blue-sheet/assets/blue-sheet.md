> **Role:** Economic Buyer (exactly one: final say, can release discretionary funds and veto), User Buyer (lives with the result; many), Technical Buyer (screens; can say no, cannot say yes), Coach (credible inside, wants you to win, gives you information you cannot get elsewhere). One person can hold two roles; one role can have several people, except Economic Buyer.
> **Degree of influence:** High, Medium or Low in *this* decision, whatever the title.
> **Mode:** Growth, Trouble, Even Keel or Overconfident, per person and per issue. Re-check after every interaction.
> **Support [V]:** -5 to +5, how strongly this person favors our SSO over the alternatives. 0 is neutral. Unknown is allowed, a guess is not.
> **Access:** Direct, Indirect (name the route), or None.
